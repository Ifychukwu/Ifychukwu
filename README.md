## Featured project: NHS A&E Analytics

An independent analysis of six years of NHS England emergency department data, from raw public files to
a published dashboard and an interactive app.

[![A&E in England, 2020 to 2026](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ifychukwu/nhs-ae-analytics/main/docs/img/overview.png)](https://ifychukwu.github.io/nhs-ae-analytics/)

- **[Dashboard](https://ifychukwu.github.io/nhs-ae-analytics/)**: attendances, 4 and 12 hour waits,
  deprivation and trust performance, 2020 to 2026.
- **[Live app](https://nhs-ae-analytics-l7ywhuw5jw7wppnatnnuic.streamlit.app/)**: explore 26 million attendances a year by complaint, age, deprivation and
  trust, or ask a question in plain English (Claude writes and runs read-only SQL; every query is shown).
- **[Code](https://github.com/Ifychukwu/nhs-ae-analytics)**: Python, SQL (SQLite), pandas,
  scikit-learn, Streamlit, Plotly.

**What it shows I can do**
- Combine four years of inconsistently formatted NHS publications into one tested database
- Find and fix errors in published data: the checks caught age-group rates in NHS England's 2025-26 file
  that did not match its own counts
- Forecast demand with a seasonal model and test it on data it had not seen
- Segment 116 trusts by pressure profile with k-means clustering
- Show inequalities: the most deprived tenth of areas attends A&E almost twice as often as the least deprived
